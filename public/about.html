About Weather Co Blog

Weather, outfits and stories from the sky.

Weather Co Blog (WC Blog) is a small community site run by Weather Co Development. You type in your US zip code to see today's weather, get a simple "what to wear" list and see any official weather alerts, then share what you're seeing with other people: posts, photos, comments and more.

What you can do here

  • Check the weather: today's forecast, what to wear, and live alerts from the National Weather Service (US only).
  • Share: write posts with photos, tag them by topic, comment, like and follow people. The Recommended feed learns what you like.
  • Make friends: send friend requests and chat privately with friends.
  • Weather Credits: play money you earn by checking in every day and keeping a streak. Spend them on badges and name colors, tip people, or trade safe images in the Image Market. Credits have no cash value and cannot be bought with real money.
  • WC Blog Premium: paid for with Weather Credits, it unlocks video posts, the animated Weather Mode background and other perks.
  • Install it: add the site to your phone's home screen to use it like an app.

How we try to keep it safe

  • Age protections: people under 13 can only join with a parent or guardian's OK, always have Restricted Mode on, and never see 13+ posts.
  • Private messages are friends-only, and young people can only be friends with other young people close to their age. Adults can only be friends with adults.
  • Reporting: every post, comment, private message and market listing has a Report button. Reported content is reviewed by the team.
  • Moderation tools: we can remove content, suspend accounts, pause features and filter banned words.
  • The Image Market is for safe, all-ages images only.
  • No ads, no selling your data. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

Where our weather information comes from

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, zip-code lookups from Zippopotam.us, and alerts from the US National Weather Service. Forecasts can be wrong. In a real emergency, follow your local officials and official alerts.
