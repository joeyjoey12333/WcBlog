Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to run the site, we never sell it, there are no ads, and you can delete your account yourself in Settings. We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.

Weather Co Blog ("WC Blog", "we", "us") is run by Weather Co Development. This policy explains what we collect and why. Questions or requests: [YOUR EMAIL].

What we collect

When you make an account

  • A username, a display name and a password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so we can't read it.
  • Your birthdate. We use it only to switch on age protections. Other people can't see it.
  • If you are under 13, whether you said a parent or guardian knows you're joining.

What you add

  • Posts, photos, videos, comments, your bio and profile picture.
  • Likes, follows, friend requests, blocks and reports you send.
  • Private messages you send to friends.
  • Image Market listings, purchases and sales.

How you use the site

  • Your Weather Credits, daily streak, shop purchases, Premium status and history of credit changes.
  • Which days you visited (used only for our counts), your notifications and your settings (theme, restricted mode, and so on).
  • Short-term security counters that include your IP address, used to stop password guessing and spam. We clear old ones out regularly.

On your device

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We save a few things in your browser's storage so the site works: your login token, theme, text size, units, your saved zip code and whether you've closed a pop-up. If you install the site as an app, your device also keeps a copy of the site's files so it opens quickly.

Your zip code

When you look up the weather, your zip code goes to our server, which turns it into a place and asks weather services for the forecast and alerts. We don't save your zip code in your account; it stays in your browser until you clear it (Settings has a button for that). Our hosting company's logs may briefly include it.

How we use it

  • To run the site: show your posts, deliver messages, count your credits.
  • To keep people safe: apply age rules, review reports, filter banned words, stop abuse and spam.
  • To understand how the site is used: simple totals such as how many people joined or visited each day.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use your information to build advertising profiles.

Who else handles your information

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores its data. Netlify can see technical information such as IP addresses in its logs.
  • Open-Meteo and the US National Weather Service receive the map coordinates of a zip code to return the weather and alerts. Zippopotam.us receives the zip code itself.
  • Google Fonts sends the site's fonts to your browser, so Google can see your IP address when a page loads.
  • We may share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Private messages

Private messages can only be read by the people in the conversation, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. We may look at messages when someone reports one (the report includes the few messages before it), when our word filter flags one for review, or when needed for safety or legal reasons. Please never share passwords, addresses, phone numbers or other personal details in messages or posts.

Public content

Posts, comments, display names, usernames, bios and profile pictures can be seen by other people. Posts rated for all ages can be seen by visitors who aren't logged in. Photos and videos you post are public in the same way. Items for sale in the Image Market show a blurred preview to everyone and the full image only to the buyer.

Children under 13

People under 13 may join only with a parent or guardian's permission, and we ask them to confirm this when they sign up. Accounts for children under 13 have extra protections: Restricted Mode is always on, 13+ posts are hidden, they can only be friends with other young people close to their age, and the Image Market is all-ages only. We don't ask children for their email address, phone number or real name, and we don't use their information for advertising.

Parents and guardians: you can ask us to see what we have about your child, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can stop us collecting more. Email [YOUR EMAIL] with your child's username. We'll ask a few questions to check the request is really from the child's parent or guardian.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your account: in Settings choose "Delete my account." We erase your posts, comments, likes, follows, friendships, private messages, listings, profile picture, Weather Credits and notifications, and we remove your birthdate and password. Your username stays reserved so nobody else can pretend to be you.
  • Other requests: email us and we'll help with access, correction or deletion.
  • Reports and safety records about conduct may be kept when needed to protect other people. Our site owner keeps private backup files; copies of deleted data disappear from backups when old backups are replaced.

Security and keeping data

We protect your information with measures such as hashed passwords, age-based safety rules and limits on repeated login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else. We keep your information while your account exists and erase it when you delete your account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll post an update on the site. The "last updated" date above always shows the current version.
